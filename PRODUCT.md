# Product

## Register
product (the portals are Operate surfaces; the public landing page is Persuade)

## Users
Patients, caretakers, doctors, hospitals and government administrators. Doctors and hospital staff work in dense, time-pressured sessions on desktop; patients and caretakers are frequently on phones.

## Product purpose
UNITED MEDICATION connects five roles through one shared health identity and connected records.
Tagline: One health ID, lifelong safety
It is a prototype, not a certified clinical system. Demo accounts hold sample data only.

## Brand personality
Exclusive, trustworthy, minimal, precise. An international healthcare technology institution, not a hospital template.

## Anti-references
Generic AI SaaS: gradient blobs, three-column icon cards on every section, glassmorphism stacks, bright yellow gold, all-black dashboards, invented statistics or testimonials.

## Design principles
1. Black frames, ivory carries, gold marks. Gold is the single accent, never the fill.
2. Clinical data is read before it is admired: clarity over decoration in portals.
3. One shell, five roles: identical chrome, different information architecture.
4. Only real information. No invented facilities, doctors, achievements or numbers.
5. Restrained motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus, ten-language interface (labels run long in some languages), reduced-motion support.
